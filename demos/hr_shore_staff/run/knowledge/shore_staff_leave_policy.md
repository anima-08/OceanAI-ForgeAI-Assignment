# Shore Staff Leave Policy

## Eligibility

Shore staff are eligible for leave after completing 6 months of service.

## Annual Leave

*   Duration: 20 days per year
*   Accrual rate: 2 days per month
*   Carry-over rule: Up to 5 annual days may carry into the next year and must be used by March 31st.

## Sick Leave

*   Duration: 5 days per year
*   Accrual rate: 0.5 days per month
*   Carry-over rule: Up to 2 sick days may carry into the next year and must be used by March 31st.

## Family Leave

*   Duration: 10 days per year
*   Accrual rate: 1 day per month

## Notice Period

*   Minimum notice period: 2 weeks for all types of leave
*   Maximum notice period: 4 weeks for annual leave and family leave

## Approval Process

*   Managerial approval required for all leave requests
*   HR approval required for exceptional cases

## Documentation

*   Leave request form must be submitted to the manager at least 1 week prior to the start of leave
*   Medical certificates are required for sick leave and family leave

## Probationary Period

*   Shore staff on probation are not eligible for annual leave during their first 6 months
*   Shore staff on probation may request sick leave, but must provide a medical certificate

## Carry-over Rule

*   Annual carry-over: Up to 5 days may be carried into the next year and must be used by March 31st.
*   Sick carry-over: Up to 2 days may be carried into the next year and must be used by March 31st.

## Appeals Process

*   Shore staff can appeal a denied leave request within 3 days of receiving the decision
*   The appeals process will be reviewed by HR, and a final decision will be made within 5 working days.
