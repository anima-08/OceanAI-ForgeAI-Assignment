# Shore Staff Travel Policy

## Eligible Travel

Shore staff are eligible to travel for business purposes within the continental United States and Canada, excluding Alaska and Hawaii. International travel is only permitted with prior approval from management.

## Approval Thresholds

* Low-risk trips (less than 3 days): No approval required

* Medium-risk trips (3-7 days): Managerial approval required

* High-risk trips (more than 7 days): Director-level approval required

## Per-Diem Rates by Region

| Region | Daily Rate | Overnight Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Northeast | $150 | $300 |
| Southeast | $120 | $250 |
| Midwest | $100 | $200 |
| West Coast | $180 | $350 |
## Accommodation Caps

* Shore staff are limited to $500 per night for overnight stays

## Receipt Rules

* All receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the trip date

## Reimbursement Deadlines

* Reimbursements will be processed on a bi-weekly basis, with payments made by the 15th and 30th of each month

## Emergency-Travel Exception

* In the event of an emergency (e.g. family member's serious illness or severe weather), shore staff may request approval for travel without prior notice. This exception does not apply to international travel.

Note: All expenses must be documented and approved in accordance with company policies and procedures.
