# Shore Staff Conduct Policy

## Purpose
This policy outlines the expected conduct of shore staff at Blue Harbour Marine Services, ensuring a safe and respectful work environment for all employees.

## Safety Conduct
Shore staff are responsible for maintaining a safe working environment. This includes:
* Following established safety procedures and protocols
* Reporting any hazards or incidents to their supervisor immediately
* Participating in regular safety training and drills
* Refraining from engaging in horseplay, pranks, or other behavior that may compromise safety

## Harassment Reporting
Blue Harbour Marine Services takes all allegations of harassment seriously. Shore staff are expected to report any incidents of harassment to HR immediately. The following steps will be taken:
* Investigation by HR and/or management
* Support for the affected employee
* Disciplinary action against the perpetrator, up to and including termination

## Alcohol and Drug Rules
The use of alcohol or drugs on company premises is strictly prohibited. Shore staff are expected to:
* Refrain from consuming alcohol or using illegal substances while on duty
* Not bring alcohol or drugs onto company premises
* Cooperate with any request for a drug test or breathalyzer test

## Grievance Steps
Shore staff have the right to report grievances to HR. The following steps will be taken:
* Initial review by HR
* Investigation and support for the employee
* Resolution of the issue, which may include disciplinary action against the perpetrator

## Disciplinary Consequences
Disciplinary consequences for shore staff will vary depending on the severity of the infraction. Repeated offenses may result in termination.

## Protected Reporting
Shore staff are protected from retaliation when reporting harassment, discrimination, or other forms of misconduct. Reports made in good faith will be kept confidential to the extent possible.

This policy is not intended to provide legal advice and should not be relied upon as such. For specific guidance, please consult with HR or management.
