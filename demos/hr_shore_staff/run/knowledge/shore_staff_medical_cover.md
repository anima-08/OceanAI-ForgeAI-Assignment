# Shore Staff Medical Cover Policy

## Eligibility Waiting Period
The waiting period for shore staff medical cover is 30 days from the date of employment.

## Inpatient/Outpatient Limits
Inpatient limits are as follows:
*   General care: 2 visits per year
*   Specialist care: 1 visit per year

Outpatient limits are as follows:
*   General care: 10 visits per year
*   Specialist care: 5 visits per year

## Dental/Vision Benefits
Shore staff are eligible for dental and vision benefits.
Dental benefits include routine cleanings, fillings, and extractions.
Vision benefits include annual eye exams and glasses or contact lenses.

## Exclusions
The following conditions are excluded from coverage:
*   Pre-existing medical conditions (except as noted below)
*   Dental work not related to oral health
*   Vision care for non-medical purposes

## Dependants
Dependants of shore staff include spouses and children under the age of 18.

## Claim Documents
Claim documents must be submitted within 60 days of the service date.

## Deadlines
Claims must be submitted by the end of the calendar year to be processed in the current year.

## Pre-authorization Rules
All medical services require pre-authorization prior to treatment.
Exceptions include emergency situations and services covered under our standard policy.

# Additional Information
Please note that this policy is subject to change without notice.
